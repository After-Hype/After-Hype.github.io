Attendance Tracker

A simple way to understand, monitor and manage your class attendance.

Plan your attendance

Stay aware. Stay on track.

Attendance Tracker helps students record attendance and understand their attendance position at a glance. Use the Calendar Dashboard to review your attendance history and the Attendance Calculator to explore how many classes you can miss or need to attend to reach your target.
