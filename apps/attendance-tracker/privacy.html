Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the Attendance Tracker Android application.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy applies to Attendance Tracker, published by AfterHype ("we", "us"). It explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Attendance information and local storage

Attendance dates and status entries that you create in the app are intended to be stored locally on your device using the app's local storage. AfterHype does not receive or maintain those attendance entries on its own server through the app. Your records may be lost if you clear app data, uninstall the app, reset your device or otherwise remove its local storage. You are responsible for keeping any backup you need.

Advertising and third-party SDKs

The app may display ads using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers or other device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and ad-related events to provide, measure, prevent fraud in and improve advertising. Depending on your settings, region and the app's ad configuration, this may include personalized advertising. Ad processing is handled by Google and its partners under their policies.

Learn more at Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Help.

Information we do not collect through an AfterHype account

Attendance Tracker does not require you to create an AfterHype account. The app is designed not to upload your attendance entries to an AfterHype server. This does not mean that third-party SDKs, such as advertising or platform services, never process device or usage information.

Permissions

The app may request permissions or use device capabilities required for its features or advertising SDK. The permissions shown by Android on your device are the most accurate description of the current installed build.

Children

Attendance Tracker is a general productivity/education utility. If you believe information relating to a child has been processed in a way that raises a privacy concern, contact us.

Data retention and your choices

Attendance records remain in local app storage until you remove them or Android removes the app's data. Advertising data handled by Google is subject to Google's retention and privacy controls. You can manage advertising settings through your Android device and Google account where available.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version will be published on this page. Contact info.afterhype@gmail.com with privacy questions.
