Privacy Policy

How AfterHype handles information across this website and its products.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

AfterHype ("we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains information that may be processed when you visit this website or contact us.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, you choose what information to include, such as your name, email address, phone number and message. The website contact form uses your device's email application to prepare a message addressed to info.afterhype@gmail.com. The website itself does not transmit the form contents to an AfterHype server or store them in a website database.

Website technical information

Our hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as IP address, browser details and request logs, to deliver and protect the website. The exact information and retention period depend on the hosting provider and its settings.

Apps and third-party services

Individual apps may use third-party services, including advertising services such as Google AdMob. Those providers may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, approximate location inferred from IP, and ad interaction information, subject to their own policies and the app's configuration. This processing is separate from any attendance or other content you enter locally in an app.

For details about a specific app, read its app-specific privacy policy linked from that app's page. Google privacy information is available at Google's Privacy Policy.

Data storage and sharing

We do not operate a central AfterHype user account or website database for the contact form. We do not sell personal information. Information you send to us by email is handled within our email service and may be retained as needed to respond, provide support or meet legal obligations. Third-party providers process data under their own terms and policies.

Your choices and rights

You can choose not to submit the contact form and can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have emailed us, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements.

Children's privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the website or our apps change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Questions can be sent to info.afterhype@gmail.com.
